Design in VC
250119

I did not plan to end up in venture capital. In fact, no one can plan for it (unless you are rich enough to start your own fund). Many people have asked me how to get into VC as a designer. And just like other Partner level roles actually, you have to know someone and you have to know the right people, mostly investors aka rich people. Then it might happen. But just designers are way more rare. There are less than 100 people out of this world that had the role of Design Partner, the same job that I held.

I was the Head of Design at a successful billion dollar startup but nobody had heard of because the work lived in unsexy domains. Biotech, gov, defense, among others. If I am doing it right, the work should not make a sound.

The company exited, the founders started a VC fund, and they simply pulled me in as a founding partner. It was luck. It was also earned. People call you when your work keeps showing up.

The best part of the job is getting to design new ventures with brilliant founders from scratch. Before the org chart even existed. But people have also asked if design belongs this early. As biased I am as a designer, I have to firmly say, no. Unless you want unfair advantage over your competitors. You can make great products that people want, but Design helps make inspiring products that make people desire. Design should be equal to technology. Design equals to technology. It turns possibilities into something real, into something people can use and can believe in.

When tech is getting easier to assemble and the bar to ship is lower, then the differentiator shifts to the experience. Consumer products reset expectations for what good feels like. Now users demand that same clarity for all sorts of product too, even for the unsexy parts of the industry. Design with a capital D is leverage. It turns complexity into trust. It turns capability into adoption.

But what a Design Partner actually do day to day you might ask, well we work upstream. Besides performing due diligence on thousands of potential investments a year on the design aspects of the business, help the portfolio companies making first design prototypes, and making countless number of pitch decks, I got to actually help incubate several exiting startups myself, and then go find someone to be the founders to run the companies. So as a fact, I am the founder of so many startups prior to the offical founders.

I help the founders clarify what they are building and why it should exist. I shape narrative and positioning so the team can make decisions faster. A good internal story becomes a decision making machine. Cool looking brand and logo included. Then we translate it into product reality; the first user, the first workflow, the first proof of concept, the first protptype, the first bit of culture.

Another great thing about this job is that it comes with so much power and authority. When you hold the cash, everybody has to listen to you (I did not abuse the power tho, for the record). I could choose which fun design problems I wanted to work on. I could say no to the boring stuff (ummm, sorry I will be at a ski resort for the next 2 weeks meeting investors, but I know just the guy who could help with your design). I could shape the design vision in whichever way I wanted, and the founders would accept it as truth (good thing that my design is not shit).

I got my hands on so many cool projects I would never imagine of. A nuclear power plant (unlimited abundant energy you say?)? Check. A hotel in space (only for millionaires)? Check. A floating city in the case that the Earth become Waterworld, and got supported from the UN? Check. It's like if I run a venture design studio, but I could pick any kind of projects I want, and never had to worry about getting customers or paying the bills.

And when the fun dies, I tap into my network and replace myself with my trusted designer friends. Of course I want to keep my talented peers in the ecosystem to help expanding the portfolio.

If you are building frontier tech in AI, defense, biotech, or govtech, or just anything cool really, we should meet. Pre hype, pre seed, pre whatever. I want to be your first believer and one of the first angel checks. If you are set to transform billions of lives, just holler.
