Lightcone Ventures
Fight Entropy, Choose Optimism

Role
Creative Director

Scope
Brand strategy
Visual identity
Art direction

Lightcone Ventures backs companies building power systems, orbital infrastructure, and advanced computing hardware among other deep tech startups. The design vision pictured what that optimism might look like on an ordinary future day.

The name gave the identity a shape that could span those industries. Logo directions explored the geometry of a light cone through wireframes, particles, and slices. The final logomark pares nineteen slices down to eleven ellipses, so it's more flexible and adaptable for various brand applications. The same ellipse acts as a graphic device to frame the imagery on marketing collaterals.
