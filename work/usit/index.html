United States Innovative Technology invests in commercial companies developing technologies of national importance. The identity conceptualizes a solid square sits beside either the full name or the four initials, giving USIT a signature that fits between a formal introduction and a ski event.

Inspired by NYC culture, it formed the blunt lettering style. The initials move to the corners, leaving the center for the person handing one over. New webpage concepts, the Field Assembly conference poster, and an investment memo extend the original work in 2026.
