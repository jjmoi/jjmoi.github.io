Cerebras builds AI infrastructure for fast training and inference. I joined as its first product designer, leading design across the developer platform, brand, and design system as the company prepared for IPO.

The challenge was to turn a complex, engineering led product into a clear platform experience that could scale across products for millions of developers.

Drawn from the physical form of Cerebras' wafer-scale chip and modular rack, the brand and product design extend that design philosophy and applied to the layout, typography, illustration, data visualization, and user interface.

The system was built to be AI-ready from the start and it became the foundation for software development. Engineering teams got the guidance, design tokens, components, and patterns to turn ideas into shipped product faster.

The underlying system powers the Cerebras Developer Console, which simplifies complex inference workflows across onboarding, observability, and deployment.

The same system also supported Cerebras Knowledge, an AI-native platform connecting docs, code, communication, and internal systems. Within three months of launch, it was answering more than 15,000 questions a day.

The work set Cerebras up to go public in one of the largest AI IPOs and making it a primary driver of the broader market's performance in 2026.

Debuted at Supernova, Cerebras' falgship event, the refreshed brand and design of the product platform moved the story beyond raw inference speed to what lightspeed AI can make possible.
