Cerebras builds AI infrastructure designed to make training and inference dramatically faster. Its purpose built hardware and cloud platform give developers and enterprises access to high performance AI compute without the complexity of managing traditional GPU infrastructure.

As the company prepared for IPO, Cerebras was evolving from an engineering led product into a more mature developer platform and brand. The opportunity was to make that technical advantage tangible through a clearer cloud experience, stronger product storytelling, and a design system that could scale with the company.
